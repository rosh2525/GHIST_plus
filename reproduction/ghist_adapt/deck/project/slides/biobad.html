Biology · limits

Where it does not help, or makes things worse

Rare signalling: invisible

Chemokines CCL20 and CCL8 (under 1% of cells) stay near PCC 0.02: transient states leave little mark on morphology.

Myeloid cells barely move

Myeloid programme gains only +0.004 to +0.006 on both slides.

A few genes get worse

CD36, TSPAN8, ITGB1: GHIST+’s on/off signal is near noise for them, so trusting it more hurts.

Maps and co-expression

Fix 1 alone blurs maps; on ATERA, HAC’s gene–gene structure is worse than the authors’ (CMD 0.319 vs 0.264).

Amount in expressing cells

All core corrections, the authors’ included, err more there than GHIST+ on Breast5K (0.71 vs 0.66).

Badly placed cores

A gate to protect tissue the cores missed failed its test; B-cell markers on Breast5K sit slightly below RRC.

Use it for: tumour, epithelial, stromal and immune-cell localisation. Be careful with: absolute expression levels, rare signalling genes and gene–gene networks.

These are the honest failure modes. Rare signalling genes such as chemokines are expressed in very few cells and transiently, so no H&E method can be expected to recover them well. The support-aware gate (P2 in our plan) failed its pre-declared test and was dropped. All core corrections, including the authors', compress expression magnitudes.
