Uncertainty that holds where a gene is expressed

A trap we found first

Standard per-gene 90% intervals report about 91% coverage, yet cover 0% of the cells that actually express the gene: most measured values are zero, so the intervals learn to cover zeros.

How much, if it is on?

90% range

Coverage on expressing cells 90.4% (Breast5K) and 89.9% (ATERA); 48% and 20% narrower than around GHIST+’s raw amount.

Protocol config_v7.json, amended after one development-slide smoke run, before any confirmation run. With tumour-only cores, coverage stays 0.89–0.91.

We report uncertainty the way the hurdle model thinks: first the probability that a gene is on, then a range for how much if it is on. GHIST+'s raw probabilities are miscalibrated; after correction with the cores, they match observed frequencies.
