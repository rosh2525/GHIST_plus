GHIST+ adds a foundation encoder, neighbour-aware graph reasoning, tissue prototypes, panel imputation and pan-cancer training. Two details from the released code matter for us: the hurdle output head, which models whether a gene is on and how much, and a post-hoc ridge correction from measured cores. Note the headline numbers are on the 20 most spatially variable genes, the easiest ones.
